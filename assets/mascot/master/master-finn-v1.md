## Purpose

Master Finn is the official reference character for the entire WordFish platform.

Every future illustration, animation, emotion, accessory and promotional artwork must be created from this reference.

## Design Rules

The following elements must NEVER change:

- body proportions
- overall silhouette
- head shape
- face proportions
- eye size
- eye spacing
- pupil style
- smile shape
- cheek style
- fin shapes
- tail shape
- dorsal fin shape
- stripe placement
- yellow dots
- outline thickness
- official colors

## Future Illustrations May Change

Only these elements may change:

- facial expression
- eye direction
- eyebrows
- fin position
- body pose
- accessories
- animation

Nothing else.

## Personality

Finn is:

- friendly
- trustworthy
- curious
- intelligent
- playful
- calm
- encouraging

Finn is never:

- angry
- aggressive
- sarcastic
- scary
- goofy
- overly childish
